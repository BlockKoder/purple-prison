Server Information

Purple Prison is a Minecraft prison server featuring a player-driven economy, custom mining progression, intense PvP combat, and unique parkour and minigame challenges. Players can mine through ranks, trade with others, upgrade their gear, and compete in various server activities.

Purple Prison is a long-standing Minecraft prison server that offers a classic yet continuously updated prison gameplay experience. Designed for players who enjoy progression, competition, and community interaction, Purple Prison combines traditional mining mechanics with modern features.

Players start at the beginning rank and work their way up by mining resources, earning money, and completing objectives. The server features a thriving player-driven economy where users can trade items, set up shops, and build wealth through smart trading and resource management.

Beyond mining, Purple Prison provides a variety of engaging activities. Combat enthusiasts can participate in high-stakes PvP battles, while players looking for alternate challenges can try parkour courses and minigames to earn extra rewards. With active community events, regular updates, and a dedicated player base, Purple Prison remains one of the premier destinations for Minecraft prison server fans.
